LaTIM-SNAP README
-----------------

**LaTIM-SNAP is a fork of ITK-SNAP.** This distribution is a community fork
of the original ITK-SNAP software (https://itksnap.org), released under the
same GNU General Public License. We are grateful to Paul A. Yushkevich and the
PICSL team at the University of Pennsylvania for creating the original
software. This fork extends ITK-SNAP with additional experimental features
(e.g. the AI assistant panel and greedy deformable registration). Please see
the Credits page in the About dialog for full attribution.

### Welcome!

Thank you for your interest in LaTIM-SNAP! By using this program you are joining
an ever growing community of users from research labs around the world.
LaTIM-SNAP is an open-source software application made possible by the
committment of dedicated developers, who often make contributions on a
volunteer basis. Original development of LaTIM-SNAP was supported by the U.S.
National Library of Medicine through PO 467-MZ-202446-1. Subsequent
development of LaTIM-SNAP version 2.0 has been supported by the U.S. National
Institute of Biomedical Imaging and BioEngineering and the NIH Blueprint for
Neuroscience through grant 1 R03 EB008200-01. Development of LaTIM-SNAP versions
3.0 - 3.6 has been supported by the NIH grant 1R01 EB014346. 

LaTIM-SNAP is the result of over 20 years of work by many researchers, software
engineers and students. Please visit the [Credits page](http:/itksnap.org/credits.php) 
to learn about the team that made LaTIM-SNAP possible!

### Getting Started 

LaTIM-SNAP was designed for ease of use. There are several resources to get
started with it. 

*   If you downloaded LaTIM-SNAP as a binary executable from
    [itksnap.org](http://www.itksnap.org/download/snap), you can get started
    by reading the [online tutorials](http://www.itksnap.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php?n=Documentation.HomePage).
*   If you downloaded LaTIM-SNAP source code, please follow these [online
    instructions](http://www.itksnap.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php?n=Documentation.BuildingLaTIM-SNAP)
    to compile and build LaTIM-SNAP.
*   You will need a 3D image to use this software. Images with which LaTIM-SNAP
    is known to work are found on the [LaTIM-SNAP download page](http://www.itksnap.org/download/snap)
*   There are also materials (images, exercises, video) from training courses
    available on the [documentation page](http://www.itksnap.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php?n=Documentation.SNAP3). 

### Asking Questions

If you have a question about LaTIM-SNAP, here are the places where you may find an answer. Keep in mind that since LaTIM-SNAP in not a commercial product, it may take some time for an answer to arrive.

*   If you have an question about how to use SNAP or why it's not doing what
    it should, post in on the [LaTIM-SNAP Users' List](http://www.itksnap.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php?n=MailingLists).
*   If you have a technical question or a questing related to ITK, join the 
    [ITK Users' Mailing List](http://www.itk.org/HTML/MailingLists.htm) and
    post your question there.
